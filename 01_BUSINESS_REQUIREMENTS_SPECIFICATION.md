# 01 - Business Requirements Specification (BRS)
## ETSWalletV2 Affiliate Management & Commission Program

---

## 1. Document Overview & Objective
This document outlines the business context, functional requirements, operational workflows, and business rules governing the Affiliate Management System in ETSWalletV2. It serves as the primary requirements baseline for Quality Assurance (QA) verification, Product Management, and Development Teams.

---

## 2. Business Context & Objectives
The Affiliate Program is designed to drive player acquisition and retention by partnering with external promoters (Affiliates). Affiliates promote the platform through unique referral links and marketing channels. In exchange, affiliates receive a share of the net gaming revenues generated by the players they refer, subject to operational expenses, fee deductions, minimum performance thresholds, and administrative approvals.

### Core Business Objectives:
1. **Automated Attribution**: Reliably link newly registered players to the referring affiliate using a transparent attribution model.
2. **Flexible Commission Structuring**: Support diverse affiliate agreements via customizable Affiliate Groups and multi-tiered commission brackets.
3. **Equitable Win/Loss Sharing**: Calculate commissions based on actual Company Gross Gaming Revenue (GGR), preventing affiliate payouts during negative net revenue periods.
4. **90-Day Staged Protection**: Implement a staged carry-forward mechanism that gives affiliates up to 90 days to achieve minimum profit thresholds before balances reset.
5. **Financial Integrity & Auditability**: Ensure all balance credits and deductions are backed by double-entry ledger transactions and strict admin approval controls.

## 4. Affiliate Onboarding & Lifecycle Management

### 4.1 Affiliate Account Creation
Affiliate accounts can be created through two pathways:
1. **Self-Registration (Affiliate Portal)**:
   - Prospective affiliate submits: Username, Email, Password, Confirm Password.
   - Password requirements: Minimum 8 characters, at least 1 uppercase letter, 1 lowercase letter, 1 digit.
   - System automatically creates an `ApplicationUser` with role `affiliate`.
   - System assigns default `CountryId = 106` and default `AffiliateGroupId = 1`.
   - System auto-generates a unique 12-character alphanumeric `AffiliateCode` formatted as `AFF{MerchantId:D3}{Random6Digits}` (e.g., `AFF001849201`).
   - Status defaults to **Active (1)**.
2. **Admin-Assisted Creation (BackOffice)**:
   - Admin creates affiliate with full profile: Country, Affiliate Group, Username, Name, Contact Number, Email, Domain, Parent Affiliate, Password.
   - System auto-generates unique 8-character alphanumeric code using `GuidHelper.GenerateAlphanumericId(8)`.

### 4.2 Account Status States
- **Active (1)**: Affiliate can log in, track clicks, receive new member attributions, and accrue commissions.
- **Inactive (2)**: Affiliate cannot log in. New member referrals using the affiliate code are ignored or blocked.

## 5. Member Referral & Attribution Rules

### 5.1 Referral Mechanism & Link Generation
- Affiliates are provided a branded referral link containing their unique Affiliate Code:
  $$\text{Referral URL} = \text{BaseMemberURL} + \text{"/?aff="} + \text{AffiliateCode}$$
  *Example*: `https://member.site.com/?aff=AFF001849201`
- Affiliates can copy this link with a single click from their Overview Dashboard or Profile page.

### 5.2 Cookie Attribution Policy (First-Touch Attribution)
- When a prospective player clicks a referral link, `AffiliateTrackingMiddleware` intercepts the request.
- The middleware inspects the query parameter `?aff={AffiliateCode}`.
- **Cookie Name**: `SiteAff`
- **Cookie Duration**: 30 Days from creation.
- **First-Touch Rule (Fairness Policy)**:
  > **Business Rule**: If a prospective player already has an existing `SiteAff` cookie, **the system does NOT overwrite it**. The attribution remains locked to the initial referring affiliate to preserve referral rights.

### 5.3 Digital Marketing & Ad Postback Integration
- Alongside `?aff=`, the system captures performance marketing tracking parameters:
  - `vcid` (Visitor Campaign ID)
  - `bcid` (Banner Click ID)
  - `rtid` (Retargeting Tracking ID)
  - `clickid` (General Ad Click Identifier)
- Captured parameters are persisted in corresponding HTTP cookies for conversion postback reporting.

### 5.4 Member Registration Binding
- During member registration on the Member Portal:
  1. The system checks if `SiteAff` cookie exists.
  2. If found, the valid `AffiliateId` matching the code is stamped onto `MemberProfile.AffiliateId`.
  3. The association between Member and Affiliate is permanent and immutable for the lifetime of that member profile.

## 6. Affiliate Groups & Governance

Affiliates are organized into **Affiliate Groups**. Affiliate Groups act as the policy template governing:
1. **Commission Settlement Period**: Length of each settlement calculation window (default: 30 days).
2. **Valid Period (Cycle Length)**: Total duration of the carry-forward rollover cycle (default: 90 days).
3. **Transaction Fee Deductions**:
   - `DepositTransactionFeeRate`: Fee percentage deducted from member deposit volumes (e.g., 1.50%).
   - `WithdrawTransactionFeeRate`: Fee percentage deducted from member withdrawal volumes (e.g., 1.00%).
4. **Member Qualification Criteria**:
   - `ActiveMemberValidTurnover`: Minimum betting turnover a member must generate to be counted as active.
   - `ActiveMemberMinDeposit`: Minimum deposit a member must execute to be counted as active.
   - `TotalCommissionActiveMembers`: Minimum active member count required before an affiliate can receive commission.
5. **Withdrawal & Deposit Limits for Affiliates**:
   - Controls minimum and maximum withdrawal amounts, daily counts, and daily limits for affiliate payouts.

## 7. Multi-Tier Commission Program Structure

Affiliate Groups utilize **Tiered Commission Settings** (`AffiliateCommissionSetting` linked to `AffiliateTier`).

### 7.1 Tier Parameters
Each tier defines:
- **Tier Name**: Identifier (e.g., "Tier 1 - Standard", "Tier 2 - VIP", "Tier 3 - Platinum").
- **Display Order**: Sorting priority.
- **Minimum Win Amount (Threshold)**: The minimum Company Gross Gaming Revenue (Company GGR) the affiliate's member pool must generate in the period to qualify for this tier.
- **Commission Rate (%)**: Percentage of the adjusted net profit paid to the affiliate (e.g., 20%, 30%, 45%).
- **Royalty Rate Win/Loss (%)**: Platform/game provider royalty percentage deducted from Company GGR (e.g., 10%).
- **Maximum Commission Amount**: Optional cap on total commission payable in a single settlement period.

### 7.2 Tier Selection Logic
Tiers within a group are evaluated in **descending order of Minimum Win Amount**:
- The system checks if the affiliate's accumulated GGR meets or exceeds the tier's `MinimumWinAmount`.
- The first (highest) tier matched is selected.
- If accumulated GGR is below the lowest tier's `MinimumWinAmount`, **no commission is generated ($0.00)**, and the GGR enters the **90-Day Carry-Forward Rollover Pipeline**.

## 9. Business Rules Summary Matrix

| ID | Rule Name | Description | Enforcement Point |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BR-01** | First-Touch Attribution | The initial referral cookie `SiteAff` is never overwritten by subsequent referral clicks within its 30-day lifespan. | `AffiliateTrackingMiddleware` |
| **BR-02** | Successful & Settled Only | Only wagers with `TransactionStatus = Success` and `SettlementStatus = Settled` qualify for GGR calculation. | `AffiliateCommissionCalculationService` |
| **BR-03** | Separated Bet Streams | Member betting volume is split into Deposit Bets and Bonus Bets. Only Deposit Bet GGR constitutes Company GGR for commission. | Data aggregation query |
| **BR-04** | Tier Threshold Requirement | If an affiliate's accumulated GGR is below the tier minimum win threshold, no commission is paid ($0.00). | Tier matching algorithm |
| **BR-05** | Expense Deductions | Deposit fee %, withdrawal fee %, and provider royalty % must be deducted from Company GGR prior to commission computation. | Commission calculation formula |
| **BR-06** | Maximum Commission Cap | If `MaximumCommissionAmount` is configured on a tier, the final commission cannot exceed this value. | Commission calculation formula |
| **BR-07** | 90-Day Carry Forward Expiry | Unmet GGR carry-forward accumulates across 3 stages (30 days each). If not qualified by the end of Stage 3, carry-forward expires. | `AffiliateCommissionCarryForwardService` |
| **BR-08** | Immediate Balance Credit | Approving a commission report immediately increases `Affiliate.Balance` and generates a persistent transaction ledger entry. | `AffiliateCommissionReportController` |
| **BR-09** | Rejection Clawback | Rejecting an already approved commission report immediately deducts the commission from `Affiliate.Balance` and records a ledger debit. | `AffiliateCommissionReportController` |
| **BR-10** | Complete Days Only | Automated cron jobs only process completed past days up to `UtcNow.Date` to avoid calculating on incomplete intra-day data. | `CalculateAffiliateCommissionJob` |
